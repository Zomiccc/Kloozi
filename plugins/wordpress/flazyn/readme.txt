Automatically send leads from WordPress contact forms to your Flazyn account.

== Description ==

Flazyn is a powerful CRM for lead management. This plugin automatically captures leads from your WordPress contact forms and sends them to your Flazyn account in real-time.

Supported Form Plugins: WPForms, Contact Form 7, Elementor Forms, Ninja Forms, Gravity Forms, Formidable Forms, Fluent Forms, Everest Forms, MetForm, Forminator, Divi Forms, Houzez.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the flazyn folder to /wp-content/plugins/
2. Activate the plugin through the Plugins menu in WordPress
3. Go to Flazyn in the admin sidebar
4. Enter your Flazyn account token (found in Flazyn then Automations then WordPress Integration)
5. Click Save Settings then Test Connection
