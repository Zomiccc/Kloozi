Automatically send leads from WordPress contact forms to your CRM Final account.

== Description ==

CRM Final is a powerful CRM for lead management. This plugin automatically captures leads from your WordPress contact forms and sends them to your CRM Final account in real-time.

Supported Form Plugins: WPForms, Contact Form 7, Elementor Forms, Ninja Forms, Gravity Forms, Formidable Forms, Fluent Forms, Everest Forms, MetForm, Forminator, Divi Forms, Houzez.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the crm-final folder to /wp-content/plugins/
2. Activate the plugin through the Plugins menu in WordPress
3. Go to CRM Final in the admin sidebar
4. Enter your CRM Final account token (found in CRM Final then Automations then WordPress Integration)
5. Click Save Settings then Test Connection
